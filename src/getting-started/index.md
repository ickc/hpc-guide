This part is for you if you have just been given an allocation on a cluster, and have only ever run your work on your own computer. It starts with what a cluster is, and what you should and shouldn't do on the node you log in to, then goes through your first batch job, how to find out what a job actually used so that the next one asks for the right resources, and a checklist for when a job fails, with where in this guide each fix is.
